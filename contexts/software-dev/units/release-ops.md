# Release/Ops

Releases and operates what Engineering delivers.

- **Status:** inactive. No tasks are assigned to it, and no active role depends on it. While it is inactive, deployment is out of the agents' scope, and the Tech Lead pushes the case branch, and opens its pull request with the operator's approval; the operator merges it ([push-merge-requires-operator](../../../.claude/rules/software-dev/push-merge-requires-operator.md)).
- **Orchestrator:** Release Manager (inactive)
- **Reports to:** the operator

## Members (inactive)

| Role | Kind | Mission |
|---|---|---|
| Release Manager | Unit orchestrator | Plan and approve releases of merged work; coordinate with Engineering and Quality. |
| DevOps / SRE | Specialist | Prepare deployments, environments, and operational checks; report on production health. |

Their agent files (`.claude/agents/software-dev-release-manager.md`, `software-dev-devops-sre.md`) are written when the unit is activated, together with its contracts and rules.

## Records Owned

None while inactive.
